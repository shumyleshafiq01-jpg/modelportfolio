The Persona

Where Haute Couture Meets Digital Influence.

Sienna Laurent is an internationally published fashion model, beauty face, and creator known for her architectural presence, effortless elegance, and modern multi-channel reach. Having walked for leading European fashion houses and fronted campaigns for premier luxury beauty houses, she brings exceptional versatility from the runway to high-converting social campaigns.

Through her brand Maison Laurent, Sienna bridges high fashion editorial standards with engaging lifestyle and beauty storytelling — offering brands an authentic conduit to hundreds of thousands of affluent, style-conscious consumers worldwide.
